Over the last year, media, marketing, content production, event promotion, and digital audience development efforts have transformed the Jeff "Big Frog" Curran brand ecosystem into an integrated media network. All properties — the Jeff Curran Invitational, @BigFrogBJJ (Jeff's personal Instagram), Team Curran, and Big Frog BJJ Retreats — operate under the Jeff Curran umbrella. YouTube spans two content pillars: JCI event coverage and the Jeff Curran: Martial Life documentary series. Across three JCI event cycles, combined peak reach increased from approximately 594,000 to more than 1.77 million impressions per event cycle — nearly tripling in less than one year. Website traffic grew +141% in impressions and +254% in clicks over the last 28 days, driven by the JCI 3 / Martial Life Episode 5 launch.
